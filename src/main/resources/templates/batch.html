Understanding Chunking and Transactions

In Spring Batch, a chunk size of 50 means the framework loops: read 1 item → process 1 item until 50 items are accumulated. Then it opens a database transaction, calls the ItemWriter with the 50 items, commits the transaction, and releases memory. If row 1,452 fails, only chunk 30 rolls back—the previous 1,400 rows are already safely committed!
